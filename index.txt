# about

About me

I'm Ajith Kumar, a Graphic Designer and Founder of Sai Graphic Designs, specializing in creative graphic design, branding, print media and digital marketing visuals.

I help businesses, startups and individuals build a strong visual identity through professional and creative designs. From logos and brand identities to social media creatives, brochures, packaging and print materials, I focus on creating designs that are attractive, meaningful and effective.

With experience working with 100+ clients, my goal is to understand each client's requirements and transform their ideas into professional visual designs that help their brand stand out.
