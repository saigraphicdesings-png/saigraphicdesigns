Ordering terms

Prices and images marked sample are illustrative. Live products can be personalised and submitted as order requests. We confirm your final design, delivery charge and payment instructions before production. Payment is arranged with the studio; this website does not collect card details. Production begins after design and payment confirmation. Delivery is offered to PIN codes where Professional Couriers or ST Courier confirms parcel delivery. Courier coverage can change and may be limited to listed localities. Delivery dates are estimates and courier progress is updated by our store. Contact us before ordering to confirm materials, finish and cancellation or replacement arrangements for personalised products. If a delivery arrives damaged, contact us with your order number and photos for review.
