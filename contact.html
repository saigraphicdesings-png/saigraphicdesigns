Chambers Operations FAQs

What is your minimum print order volume? +
We maintain a minimum print volume threshold of 10 items for custom screen layouts. Individual customized prints can be ordered through our digital customizer.
Do you offer sample fabrics before bulk printing? +
Yes. Corporate accounts can coordinate a sample pack delivery detailing cotton weaves, fabric thicknesses, and color prints options.
What file formats do you accept for custom graphics? +
We support high-resolution vector assets (SVG, AI, PDF, EPS) for screen prints. Raster file types (PNG, JPEG) must carry a resolution metric above 300 DPI.
