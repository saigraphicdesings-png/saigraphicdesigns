About Me

I'm Ajith Kumar, a Graphic Designer and Founder of Sai Graphic Designs, based in Madurai, Tamil Nadu.

I specialize in creative graphic design, branding, print media, social media creatives and digital marketing visuals.

Since 2021, I have worked with 100+ clients and helped businesses, startups and individuals build strong visual identities through professional and creative design solutions.

My work includes logo design, brand identity, social media posters, brochures, packaging, visiting cards and complete print media designs.

My goal is simple — understand the client's vision, transform ideas into powerful visuals and create designs that help brands stand out.
