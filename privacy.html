Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Sai Graphic Designs handles information submitted through customer accounts and website enquiries.

Information we collect

We may collect your name, email address, mobile number, account login details, enquiry information, and details needed to provide design or printing services.

How information is used

Information is used to provide account access, respond to enquiries, communicate about projects and orders, provide eligible downloads, and maintain website security.

Account security

Passwords are stored as password hashes rather than readable passwords. Customer sessions are used to keep you signed in securely.

Sharing

We do not publish your customer account details. Information may be shared only when needed to provide a requested service, comply with applicable law, or protect the security of the website and its users.

Retention

Account and service information may be retained while your account is active and for a reasonable period needed for business, security, and legal purposes.

Your choices

You can contact us if you want help reviewing or correcting your customer information.

Contact

For privacy questions, please contact Sai Graphic Designs.
