Ordering terms

Prices and images marked sample are illustrative. Live products can be personalised and submitted as order requests. Contact us before ordering to confirm materials, finish and cancellation or replacement arrangements for personalised products. If a delivery arrives damaged, contact us with your order number and photos for review.
