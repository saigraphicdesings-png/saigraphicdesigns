A clear remote design workflow

Planning your first project? Read how to hire a graphic designer online for your US business.

  1. Send your brief. Include the business name, approved text, references, dimensions, required files, deadline and US time zone.
  2. Agree on the quote. Confirm scope, billing currency, payment method, delivery dates and included revisions.
  3. Review the proofs. Send consolidated feedback online. Meeting availability and feedback windows are agreed for your time zone.
  4. Receive approved files. Digital artwork is delivered in the agreed formats after approval and the agreed payment steps.

For US print projects, send the printer’s specifications before artwork begins. We can work with dimensions in inches or millimeters as required by your supplier.

QUESTIONS BEFORE YOU START

Online design FAQs for US clients

Can I hire your graphic designer from the USA?

Yes. US businesses can send a brief and review artwork online. Sai Graphic Designs operates from Madurai, India, and delivers approved digital design files remotely.

What does an online graphic design project cost?

Pricing depends on the scope, number of designs, required file formats and deadline. Request an itemized quote and confirm the billing currency, payment method and included revisions before work begins.

How do time zones and revisions work?

Include your US time zone and preferred contact window in your enquiry. We agree on feedback deadlines, available meeting times and the revision scope before starting; you can review proofs asynchronously.

Can you prepare artwork for my US printer?

Yes, when included in the project scope. Send your printer’s dimensions, bleed, safe area, color profile, PDF requirements and packaging dieline before design begins. Production specifications are confirmed with your printer.

Will I receive editable source files?

Deliverables are agreed in the quote. Tell us whether you need CorelDRAW, Illustrator or Photoshop source files, print-ready PDF, SVG, PNG or JPG exports. Availability depends on the project and software used.

Do you have a US office or provide US printing delivery?

Our physical studio is in Madurai, India. This service covers remote design and digital file delivery. Physical printing and international shipping require a separate availability check and quotation.
