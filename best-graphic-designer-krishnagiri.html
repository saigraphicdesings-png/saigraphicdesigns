This guide uses two illustrative project briefs to show what to look for. They are planning examples, not claims about completed clients or a ranking of district designers.

Project example: A component supplier’s capability profile

A capability profile should explain what the company supplies, show relevant product categories and give a clear route to an enquiry. Provide approved process and specification details instead of asking the designer to infer technical capabilities from photographs.

What to check in the proof

Separate certifications, customer references and general marketing statements. Use only information the company is permitted to publish, and check every technical label in the proof.

A different brief: A produce brand’s sales sheet

A simple sales sheet can pair pack photographs with product names, quantities and ordering information. Decide which information changes seasonally and keep it in an updateable part of the layout.

Build a useful brief for your Krishnagiri project

Start with the audience and the action you want the design to support. Then list each deliverable: size, number of pages or variants, language versions and intended use. Supply approved text and the clearest available logo or product images.

  • Language: Tell us whether you need Tamil, English or separate versions. Have a fluent reviewer approve spelling, names and technical wording.
  • Production: Send your printer’s template or dieline when relevant. Ask for a realistic size proof before approving artwork.
  • Budget: Compare the same scope, revision rounds and file formats across quotes. Additional items and a new design direction should be agreed separately.
  • Handover: Identify the editing software your team uses and the export formats your supplier needs. Editable-file delivery depends on the agreed package.

Questions from businesses planning work in Krishnagiri

Should confidential customer names appear in a profile?

Include customer names or logos only when your business has permission. A capability-led layout can explain the service without exposing confidential work.
