Packaging and label design in Madurai

Sai Graphic Designs offers product packaging and label design from Madurai. Provide the printer's dimensions or dieline, approved product text and brand assets to request an accurate quote.

What we need to begin

  • Packaging type: box, pouch, bottle label or another format.
  • Exact dimensions and the printer's dieline where applicable.
  • Your logo, brand colours and product images.
  • Approved ingredients, composition, directions and required declarations.
  • Barcode or QR code artwork and finishing requirements.

Review before production

Check spelling, quantities, alignment and the placement of folds and seams. Confirm print specifications with your printer and approve a proof before production. You are responsible for confirming the accuracy of supplied product information and required declarations.

Can you design different pack sizes?

Share every required size and variant in the brief. Adaptations may require separate layouts; confirm the scope, price and delivery schedule before starting.

Artwork and final files

Agree on editable files, print PDF settings and any separate masks for special finishes with the designer and printer.
